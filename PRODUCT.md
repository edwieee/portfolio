# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: static HTML and CSS, chosen to keep this single-page portfolio lightweight, portable, and easy to deploy.

## Users

Potential collaborators, clients, and employers evaluating Edwin Biju as an independent AI engineer and builder.

## Product Purpose

Introduce Edwin, communicate his focus and technical background, and provide direct paths to contact him or view his work.

## Positioning

Edwin builds AI-powered products, intelligent agents, and practical systems beyond simple API integrations.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- A responsive, single-page introduction.
- Exact supplied biography copy must be preserved.
- Email, social profile, contact, and work links must be functional.
- No additional portfolio sections or invented claims.

## Brand Commitments

The public name is “edwin.” The voice is understated, confident, direct, and technically grounded. The supplied reference image is binding for layout, hierarchy, color, and restraint.

## Evidence on Hand

The user supplied final biography copy and a visual reference. No testimonials, project case studies, client logos, or performance claims were supplied and none may be fabricated. Edwin’s public GitHub profile provides the profile photo and verified Twitter handle.

## Product Principles

- Let concise, factual writing carry the introduction.
- Make contacting Edwin immediate.
- Keep the page focused on a single first impression.
- Preserve readability and hierarchy across desktop and mobile.
