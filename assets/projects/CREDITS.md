# Project imagery and type

The LORE plate adapts *Base of the brain*, a stipple engraving by Neele & Son, from the [Wellcome Collection](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/scdptg4t), reference 563804i, Public Domain Mark. The transparent crop comes from LORE. The portfolio recolors the engraving.

The three LORE application views are WebP editions of the landing, knowledge map and source-document captures in the [LORE project guide](https://github.com/edwieee/lore/tree/7068462e4115326bc1b5e3107d0ad7cb03f1ebdb/docs/screenshots). They show a local verification workspace. The landing capture is cropped to the opening viewport.

The five concept plates are original mathematical line illustrations. The pulse plate is illustrative and contains no recorded biometric data.
