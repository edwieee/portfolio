Upcoming concept / Video forensics

AI Deepfake Detector

Where does a clip deserve a closer look?

A proposed review desk for examining video authenticity one segment at a time.

Review brief

An analyst would open a clip and inspect intervals with inconsistent visual signals. Original frames and quality notes would stay alongside each finding so a reviewer could judge how much weight to give it.

Planned features

A frame timeline would link each observation to its timecode. Side-by-side crops could expose changes between adjacent frames. Reviewers could mark a segment inconclusive when compression, motion or lighting makes the evidence too weak.

Proposed architecture

A decoder would preserve timestamps while a worker extracts aligned face regions. Independent temporal checks would produce observations with quality flags. A report builder would group those observations by interval for human review.
